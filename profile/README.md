## What We Build

- **WhatsApp Business Platform & Automation**: official WhatsApp Business Platform integrations as a Verified Meta Tech Provider: AI virtual agents, template messaging and broadcasts, agent routing and human handover.
- **Custom Web Applications**: Laravel and Vue.js admin dashboards, internal portals, multi-tenant SaaS and API-first integrations.
- **Odoo**: Odoo implementation, upgrades and migrations as an Odoo Partner.
- **Cross-Platform Mobile Apps**: Flutter and React Native apps for iOS and Android, offline-first with GPS and camera integration, and PWAs.
- **POS & Inventory**: multi-outlet Point-of-Sale with stock sync and back-office reporting.
- **Content & Engagement Platforms**: event ticketing with QR check-in, live Q&A and polling, booking engines and headless CMS builds.

**Our stack:** Laravel · Vue.js · Go · Python · Flutter · React Native

## How We Work

1. **Discovery**: we agree on requirements, constraints and what success looks like.
2. **Design**: wireframes, user flows and data models, approved before development starts.
3. **Development**: iterative sprints with staging demos and quality assurance in each sprint.
4. **Deployment**: go-live with monitoring, staff training and post-launch support.
